About the Blue Lock Anime

Blue Lock was adapted into anime by Eight Bit studio and premiered in October 2022. The adaptation covers the manga's early arcs with strong fidelity, and the production quality — particularly the match sequences — drew immediate attention for how well it translated Blue Lock's spatial awareness framework from the page into animation. The series quickly became one of the most discussed anime of its season and built a massive global audience that extended well beyond typical sports anime fans.

The voice cast is particularly strong. Yoichi Isagi is voiced by Kazuki Ura, whose performance captures both Isagi's early hesitancy and his growing competitive conviction. Rin Itoshi is voiced by Tasuku Hatanaka. The opening and ending themes added to the series' atmosphere and became standalone hits among anime music fans.
